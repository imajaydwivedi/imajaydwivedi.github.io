Resume Tailor LaTeX

Rule-based. No AI, no invented skills. Your resume is only reordered and given a Core Competencies line built from keywords you already have, plus any gaps you confirm.

1. Resume (.tex)

2. Job description

3. Keywords

Already in your resume included automatically

Missing from your resume tick only what is true of you

Unticked keywords stay out of the resume.

Fit into your experience optional, but recommended

For each keyword you ticked, write one bullet describing work you actually did, and pick the job it belongs to. The tool inserts your wording as written. It does not write bullets for you, and each bullet must contain its keyword.
